## How was the data collected?

All data published on this website was collected **entirely through manual research**. No web scraping, automated scripts, bots, artificial intelligence, or other automated collection methods were used at any stage of the process.

Every job posting was individually reviewed by hand, and all collection activities were performed in accordance with the respective website's Terms of Service. No technical restrictions were bypassed, and no unauthorized access was performed.

## Is the information on this website official?

This website is an independent research project designed to analyze cybersecurity certification requirements across the job market. The original job postings remain the authoritative source for each position.

<br />

## How often is the data updated?

The dataset is updated regularly as new job postings are reviewed and existing information is verified. Because all research is performed manually, updates prioritize accuracy and consistency over speed. It is a very time0heavy process, do do not expect an update each week.

<br />

## Why are some certifications missing?

Only certifications that are **explicitly required or explicitly preferred** within a job posting are included.

General statements such as:

- "Relevant cybersecurity certifications"
- "Industry certifications preferred"
- "Cloud certifications"
- "Equivalent certification"

are intentionally excluded to ensure the dataset remains objective and evidence-based.

<br />

## Why are frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001, GDPR, or OWASP not listed as certifications?

These are standards, frameworks, regulations, or methodologies-not professional certifications.

The project focuses exclusively on **named professional certifications** requested by employers.
